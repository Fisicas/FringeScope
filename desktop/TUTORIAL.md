# Guided tutorial

Click Guided tour in the header. The controls panel is replaced by a tour panel that states what changed at each step and lists numbered places to look; the same numbers mark the plots and source buttons, and clicking an item outlines its plot. Use Next and Back to move between steps. The tour temporarily applies reproducible defaults. Finish or End tour restores previous input controls, time/wavenumber selections, fit-offset choice, selected tab and the exact saved fit (including optimized lifetimes and signed DAS). Theme is retained. Tour steps are single-source examples rather than additions to previously enabled sources. Manual pan/zoom and any edits made during the tour are not saved as a separate project.

1. Ideal: inspect 100 µs and 1697 cm⁻¹. The artifact-free and applied curves overlap to roundoff. Click the contour to explore other native bins. The persistent residual is part of the synthetic input, not an instrumental artifact.
2. Mirror: inspect a pure 2 nm/840 Hz motion with −100° temporal phase and 25 nm common bias. Compare kinetics and projected DAS. In Demonstrations, compare zero bias and common bias; a pure drive can yield a doubled spectral response.
3. Globar: inspect continuous leakage at fraction 0.1 with zero blocking OD and no changing-leak term. Optical contributions shows the reference/intensity change. Stable leakage can dilute true differences; it does not create a transient from an unchanged sample.
4. Actinic: inspect the early 3 µs bin at the nominal 532 nm alias near 1945.86 cm⁻¹. An assumed OPD-modulated return is enabled at detector-equivalent pulse area 2 × 10⁻⁶ IFG-peak × µs, chosen for readable comparison rather than calibrated laser fluence. Pulse width does not set spatial alias position. A 100 µs slice would miss the prompt response. Direct bypass is a separate physical hypothesis.
5. Finish: restore the original workspace. Optional: Demonstrations → Coaddition and phase compares integer-cycle timing, phase walk and 180° pairing. Coadd intensity before taking absorbance; no blanket 1/√N suppression is assumed.

All spectra are synthetic PSI-inspired ΔA. S₀ is a separate assumed single-beam reference. No experimental arrays or alternative sample selector are provided. Settings, signs, masks and units are exported; appearance does not change numerical data. The theory reader explains approximation limits and literature evidence. Larger physical displacement phase, unknown response/filtering and actual instrument phase processing require separate validation.
